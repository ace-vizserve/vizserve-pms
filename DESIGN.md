## Mission

Implementation-ready, token-driven UI guidance for VizServe PMS — optimized for consistency, accessibility, and fast delivery across an internal ops platform that also exposes two unauthenticated client screens.

## Brand

- Product/brand: VizServe PMS
- Audience: authenticated operators working queues all day (member → team_leader → manager → admin), plus unauthenticated clients who see only `/f/[slug]` and `/approve/[token]`
- Product surface: dashboard web app + two public form/approval pages
- Provenance: brand pair is D11, confirmed by Q15 on 30 Jul 2026. Foundations (type, radius, elevation, motion) are the `app.tarsi.cloud` extraction, recoloured to VizServe.

## Style Foundations

- Visual style: structured, dense, implementation-first — information before decoration. **Depth is outward only:** a raised thing carries a top highlight and casts a shadow; everything else is flat, told apart by its fill and its border. Nothing is ever embossed inward. See §1.5 of the SKILL.
- Main font style: `font.family.primary=Figtree`, `font.family.stack=var(--font-figtree)`, `font.size.base=16px`, `font.weight.base=450`, `font.lineHeight.base=1.55`, `font.tracking.base=-0.005em`
  - Loaded as a **variable** font (no `weight` array in `app/layout.tsx`). Weight 450 is the body default and is not one of the static cuts, so listing them would silently snap every screen to 400 or 500.
  - Figtree replaced Inter Tight, which replaced Geist. The token indirection is why that was a three-line change each time.
  - The family is still reachable only through `--font-sans`, `--font-heading`, `--font-display`, so changing it is a three-token edit. No component names a family.
- Typography scale: `font.size.2xs=12px`, `font.size.xs=13px`, `font.size.sm=14px`, `font.size.base=16px`, `font.size.lg=18px`, `font.size.xl=24px`, `font.size.2xl=32px`
  - The canvas drew a 13px base; it was built, read as tiny, went to 15px, read as tiny again, and landed at 16px. Do not shrink it — tighten padding instead. Retuning the tokens is what carries a type decision to ~30 pages without editing one of them — every existing `text-sm` and `text-xs` call site lands on the new size untouched. `text-3xl`+ keeps Tailwind's defaults and appears only on the marketing and sign-in surfaces.
- Radius: `--radius=0.625rem` (10px) → sm=6 · md=8 · lg=10 · xl=14. Panels `rounded-lg`, controls `rounded-md`, status chips `rounded-sm`.
- Color palette (light / dark — the values `app/globals.css` ships; full table in the SKILL §1.1):
  - `color.brand=#4359A5` · `color.brand.foreground=#FFFFFF` · `color.brand.tint=#5BC0DE` · `color.brand.ink=#202020`
  - `color.accent=#EDF0F8 / #1E2537` · `color.accent.foreground=#4359A5 / #8098DE` · `color.accent.border=#C6D0E9 / #37405C`
  - `color.background=#F5F7FA / #12151C` · `color.card=#FFFFFF / #181C25` · `color.muted=#F0F3F7 / #1E232E` · `color.track=#E7EBF1 / #262C38`
  - `color.text.primary=#0F1626 / #EDF0F5` · `color.text.secondary=#556074 / #9AA4B6` · `color.text.tertiary=#656F82 / #8E98AA` · `color.text.faint=#818B9C / #6E7889` (**non-text only**)
  - `color.border=#E3E7EE / #262C37` · `color.input=#D2D8E2 / #333A47` · `color.border.strong=#B9C1CE / #454D5C`
  - Status, each a solid + `-subtle` fill + `-border` hairline: `success=#1C7A52 / #4CB483` · `warning=#8A6206 / #D8A94A` · `info=#277590 / #5FB6D2` (darkened from the canvas #2A7F9C, which measured 3.99:1 as chip text) · `destructive=#B3352C / #E0736A`
- Spacing scale: Tailwind v4 defaults, 4px step. The extraction's 2px-step scale was rejected — a 2px increment is below the resolution at which anyone can keep spacing consistent by hand.
- Depth tokens — **outward only. Nothing is embossed inward**:
  - `--hl` / `--hl-soft` — the lit top edge of anything raised
  - `--elev-1/2/3` — what a control, a panel and an overlay cast
  - Grades: `--gradient-surface`, `--gradient-raised`, `--gradient-primary`, `--gradient-chip`, `--gradient-ambient`, consumed through the `grade-*` utilities — **never named `bg-*`**, because tailwind-merge groups every `bg-…` class and silently drops the colour beside the grade.
  - There is deliberately **no** `--well` and **no** `--pressed`. Inset shadows were tried and removed: they read as damage on a light UI and made the active sidebar row look stamped into the panel. A press is the lift collapsing (`active:shadow-none`), not the surface caving in. A field is a flat tinted panel with a border, not a carved slot.
- Shadow tokens, composed from the above so the dark theme re-points one set of values and every token follows: `shadow.ring`, `shadow.raised`, `shadow.raised-lg`, `shadow.raised-ring`, `shadow.overlay`, `shadow.chrome`
- Layout: `PageShell` is FULL WIDTH, `p-5`, `gap-4`. Two capped versions (centred, then left-aligned at 1440px) were built and reverted — a stretched table is a table problem, not a page problem. `SIDEBAR_WIDTH` is 19rem (304px), widened three times as the type grew. Top bar is 56px.
- Control scale: buttons 40/36/44/28 (default/sm/lg/xs), inputs and select triggers 40, table header 40, sidebar nav rows 40, status chips 28, icon tiles 32.
- Motion tokens: `motion.duration.instant=150ms`, `motion.duration.fast=200ms`

## The two brand laws

**`#4359A5` is the workhorse** — primary buttons, active nav, links, focus rings, headings.

**`#5BC0DE` is a surface colour, never a text colour.** 2.09:1 on white in both directions. It takes `--brand-ink` (`#202020`, 7.79:1) on top and nothing else. Its one text use is the sign-in hero heading, on the dark `--brand-gradient`, where it is large text at 3.13:1.

Where a legible teal *is* wanted, that is `--info` (`#277590`), which is a separate token and is **not** a re-pointing of `--brand-tint`.

## Accessibility

- Target: WCAG 2.2 AA
- Keyboard-first interactions required
- Focus-visible rules required — 2px `--ring` outline at 2px offset, set globally, never removed
- Contrast constraints required
- State is never conveyed by colour alone

## Where components come from

The primitives are **shadcn on `@base-ui/react`** (`components.json` sets `"style": "base-nova"`), vendored into `components/ui/`. Reach for them in this order: an existing primitive → an existing shared component in `components/` → a new primitive wrapping Base UI → anything else, last.

Full rules and the pattern for authoring a new one are in **§2 of the SKILL**.

## Rules: Do

- Use semantic tokens, not raw hex values, in component guidance.
- Use the vendored primitive for every interactive element. A native `<button>`, `<input>`, `<select>` or `<textarea>` has no tokens, no dark mode, no focus ring and no Base UI keyboard behaviour.
- Build a genuinely new primitive the way the vendored ones are built: wrap Base UI, `cva` for variants, `data-slot` on the root, `cn(...)` last, semantic tokens only, all seven states.
- Wrap any third-party component in `components/ui/` so no page imports it directly and it can be swapped without touching a screen.
- Every component must define states for default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, and error.
- Component behavior should specify responsive and edge-case handling.
- Interactive components must document keyboard, pointer, and touch behavior.
- Accessibility acceptance criteria must be testable in implementation.

## Rules: Don't

- Do not allow low-contrast text or hidden focus indicators.
- Do not use a bare HTML control in a page.
- Do not run `npx shadcn add` against the default registry — it is Radix, and it will break the shell.
- Do not name a gradient utility `bg-*`; tailwind-merge will eat the colour beside it.
- Do not use an inset shadow. Depth is outward only.
- Do not introduce one-off spacing or typography exceptions.
- Do not use ambiguous labels or non-descriptive actions.
- Do not ship component guidance without explicit state rules.

## Guideline Authoring Workflow

1. Restate design intent in one sentence.
2. Define foundations and semantic tokens.
3. Define component anatomy, variants, interactions, and state behavior.
4. Add accessibility acceptance criteria with pass/fail checks.
5. Add anti-patterns, migration notes, and edge-case handling.
6. End with a QA checklist.

## Required Output Structure

- Context and goals.
- Design tokens and foundations.
- Component-level rules (anatomy, variants, states, responsive behavior).
- Accessibility requirements and testable acceptance criteria.
- Content and tone standards with examples.
- Anti-patterns and prohibited implementations.
- QA checklist.

## Component Rule Expectations

- Include keyboard, pointer, and touch behavior.
- Include spacing and typography token requirements.
- Include long-content, overflow, and empty-state handling.
- Known surface inventory: 23 pages (16 authenticated, 7 public/auth), 26 vendored primitives, 12 shared components, ~30 co-located client components.

## Quality Gates

- Every non-negotiable rule must use "must".
- Every recommendation should use "should".
- Every accessibility rule must be testable in implementation.
- Teams should prefer system consistency over local visual exceptions.

**Two deviations from the design canvas, both forced by these numbers:**

1. **Tertiary ink.** The canvas used `#818B9C` for hints, labels, meta rows and table headers. It measures **3.44:1** and fails as text. `--muted-foreground` is `#656F82` (**5.06:1**) instead; `#818B9C` survives as `--foreground-faint` for decoration only.
2. **The info teal.** The canvas used `#2A7F9C`, which measures **3.99:1** on its own `--info-subtle` fill — under the bar for chip text. Darkened along the same hue to `#277590` (**4.57:1**).

`--brand-tint` (`#5BC0DE`) was deliberately **not** re-pointed to the info teal. `app/login/page.tsx` renders a heading in it on `--brand-gradient` at 3.13:1, which passes as large text; the darker teal on that dark blue ground would fail badly.

Status chips do not rely on their fill for the 3:1 boundary rule: each carries a hairline border, a leading dot, and its label, so state survives greyscale and print.

### Status as a SURFACE — the board columns and the list's stage groups

Added 18 Aug 2026 with the grouped task views. `taskStatusSurface()` in `components/status-badge.tsx` washes a container in the status' own tone; the tone→colour mapping stays in that file, which is the only place a status becomes a colour.

The wash carries **two alphas**, and the dark one is a measurement, not a taste call. The dark `-subtle` fills sit at almost exactly `--card`'s luminance, so a 45% wash in dark put a card at **1.00–1.04:1** against the column holding it — the two collapsed into one field. At 20% the column reads as a *hue* rather than as a lightness step and card contrast returns to **1.04–1.06:1**, level with the plain `bg-muted` column it replaced (**1.08:1**) and with `--card` on `--background` everywhere else in the app (**1.07:1**).

| Pair | Light | Dark | Needs | Verdict | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--muted-foreground` on the tone wash | **4.55–4.82:1** | **5.9–6.2:1** | 4.5:1 | pass | the count beside a column or group heading |
| `--card` on the tone wash | 1.09–1.11:1 | 1.04–1.06:1 | — | n/a | a raised card; told apart by its border and `shadow-raised`, never by fill (§1.5) |
| tone `-border` vs `--background` | **1.34–1.44:1** | **1.46–1.83:1** | — | firmer than `--border` (1.16 / 1.30) | the column edge |

Wash alphas: `bg-<tone>-subtle/45 dark:bg-<tone>-subtle/20`; brand is `bg-accent/60 dark:bg-accent/30`; neutral stays the opaque `bg-muted`. Borders are full strength.

**The wash is never the carrier.** Every column and every group is headed by a full status chip with its stage glyph, its label and its count — the tint only tells the eye where one container stops and the next begins, and the view is entirely usable in greyscale without it.
